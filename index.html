DocuFlow — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

DocuFlow is a document reader, converter and PDF creator app developed by RWB Studio. This policy explains what information the app handles. By using DocuFlow, you agree to this policy.

1. No account, no personal data collection

DocuFlow does not require you to sign in or create an account. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos, or any other personal information. We do not run analytics, advertising SDKs, or user tracking tools.

2. Your documents stay on your device

Files you open, create, convert, or share are processed locally on your device. DocuFlow does not upload your documents to our servers, and we do not operate servers that store your files. The app only requests storage access to read files you explicitly select and to save files you create.

3. Internet permission and third-party service

The app requests the INTERNET permission only to load fonts through Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com / fonts.gstatic.com). When fonts are requested, Google may receive standard technical request data, such as your IP address and user agent, under Google's Privacy Policy. No document content or personal information is sent with these requests.

4. No ads, no sale of data

DocuFlow contains no ads and does not sell or share your data with third parties for marketing.

5. Children

DocuFlow is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

6. Security

Because documents are processed on your device and are not sent to us, their security depends on your device's own lock screen and storage settings.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this page if the app changes. The updated date will always appear at the top.
